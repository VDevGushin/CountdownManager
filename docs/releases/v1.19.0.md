plain-text fragment can be reviewed and confirmed as a local `.txt` attachment. Saved links
have their own section and open in the default browser. Existing editor Save/Cancel and

and preserves the existing timer. [Russian notes](v1.19.0-notes.md) and [embedded HTML](v1.19.0-notes.html).
